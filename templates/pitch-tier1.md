# Pitch Script — Tier 1 (No Website)

**Target:** Professional services businesses (doctors, dentists, spas, physios) with zero web presence.  
**Channel:** Phone call or in-person drop-in.  
**Goal:** Book a follow-up conversation — not close on first contact.

---

## Opening (phone)

> "Hi, I'm calling for [Business Name]. My name is Jamal, I'm with Digital Disconnections — we work with local businesses in Tucson on their web presence. I noticed you're on Google Maps and have some great reviews, but you don't have your own website yet. I'd love to take 5 minutes to tell you what that's costing you — is now a good time?"

**If they say no / ask to call back:** Get a specific time. "When's a better time — morning or afternoon?" Don't leave it open-ended.

---

## The Hook (30 seconds)

> "Here's the thing — when someone finds you on Google and sees you have no website, about 40% of them leave without calling. That's real patients / clients you're not seeing. We build websites for local professionals that you *own outright* — no monthly platform fees, no subscription, no data going to Google or Wix. One time, and it's yours."

**Key phrases:**
- "You own it outright" — not Wix, not Squarespace, not their data
- "No monthly fee" — contrast with DIY platforms
- "Your clients' information stays with you" — privacy angle, relevant for medical/wellness

---

## The Objection Conversation

**"We get enough business by referral."**
> "That's a great position to be in. A website doesn't replace referrals — it just makes sure you're not losing the people who found you on Google and couldn't figure out how to reach you. It takes about 2 minutes for a new patient to decide whether to call."

**"We tried a website before and it didn't do much."**
> "Usually that's a platform problem, not a website problem. Sites built on Wix or Squarespace load slowly and Google penalizes them in search. We build them differently — and we're not locking you into anything."

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "It depends on what you need, but for a clean professional site for a [category] practice, most clients pay a one-time fee in the $500–$1,500 range. No monthly bill after that." *(Adjust as pricing is confirmed.)*

**"Can you send me something?"**
> "Absolutely. What's the best email? I'll send you three examples of what we've done for similar practices."  
> *(Note: have 3 portfolio examples ready before batch outreach begins.)*

---

## Close

Goal is NOT to sell on the first call. Goal is:
1. A follow-up call with decision-maker present (if first contact was a receptionist)
2. An email address to send examples to
3. A 15-minute Zoom or in-person walk-through

> "I'm going to be in the [Catalina Foothills / Oro Valley] area next week. Would it make sense to pop by and show you a quick demo — takes about 15 minutes?"

---

## Notes for This Batch

- **Bone** (doctor, Catalina Foothills) has no phone in database — walk-in or find number directly.

- **Spas/salons:** Lead with aesthetics and ownership. "Your brand, your site, your data."
